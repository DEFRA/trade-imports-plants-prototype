What are you importing?

Questions on this page
  • Commodity lines (a list: the questions below are asked for each one)
  • Category (must be answered, for each entry in the list)
  • Genus (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Plants for planting”, “Trees for planting”, “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane that keeps all or part of its natural round surface” or “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane in chips not processed into pellets or briquettes”
  • Species (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Plants for planting” or “Trees for planting”
  • Commodity code (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Plants for planting”, “Trees for planting”, “Conifer wood with bark, or isolated bark of conifer”, “Conifer wood without bark (from Italy, France, Portugal or Spain)”, “Cut coniferous trees more than 3 metres high”, “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane that keeps all or part of its natural round surface” or “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane in chips not processed into pellets or briquettes”
  • Quantity (must be answered, for each entry in the list)
  • Variety (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Seed potatoes” or “Ware potatoes”
  • Intended use (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Seed potatoes” or “Ware potatoes”
  • EPPO code (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Plants for planting” or “Trees for planting”
  • Size of the trees (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Trees for planting” or “Cut coniferous trees more than 3 metres high”
  • Phytosanitary treatments applied (must be answered, for each entry in the list): only if the category is “Conifer wood with bark, or isolated bark of conifer”, “Conifer wood without bark (from Italy, France, Portugal or Spain)”, “Cut coniferous trees more than 3 metres high”, “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane that keeps all or part of its natural round surface” or “Wood of sweet chestnut, ash or plane in chips not processed into pellets or briquettes”
